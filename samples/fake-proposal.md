1. Introduction

The ExampleComp Data Storytelling Workshop is an open workshop for students who want to present numbers clearly in business case competitions. Many teams collect good data but lose judges' attention because their charts do not support one clear message. In this session, a mentor shows how to choose the one number that matters, turn it into a simple chart, and build a short story around it. Participants then practise on a small case in teams and receive feedback on their slides.

2. Objectives

2.1 Teach participants how to pick the key number that supports their main argument.
2.2 Show simple rules for clear charts in competition slides.
2.3 Give participants hands-on practice turning a data table into a one-slide story.
2.4 Connect club members with students who are new to case competitions.
